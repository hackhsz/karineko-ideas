# Karineko Idea Board

Public, read-only copy of Karineko Vintage's content idea board: a daily row of jewellery and beauty
videos (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, Threads) and the ideas the team has saved.

**View it:** https://hackhsz.github.io/karineko-ideas/

**Suggest an idea:** [open the form](https://github.com/hackhsz/karineko-ideas/issues/new?template=idea.yml).
Suggestions are added to the board each morning and the issue is closed.

The page and `data/` are rebuilt every morning by a scheduled job; edits made here by hand are overwritten.
Videos and pins belong to their creators; the board only links to them.
